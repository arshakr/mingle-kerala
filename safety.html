How to Report Abuse

Report any user who makes you uncomfortable, sends inappropriate content, or appears to be underage.

  • Open the chat with the user you want to report
  • Tap the ⋯ menu button in the top-right corner
  • Select "Report Abuse"
  • Choose the reason and submit

Reports are reviewed within 24–48 hours. You may also block the user immediately.

💡

Safety Tips

  • Never share your real name, phone number, or address
  • Don't meet anyone you've only spoken to online without telling someone
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, exit immediately
  • Use the Emergency Exit button (🚨) to instantly leave any chat
  • Screenshots are discouraged — your conversations are private
  • Report users who ask you to move to another platform immediately
  • Never send money or financial details to anyone
🔐

Built-in Privacy Features

  • Anonymous username: Generated randomly, not linked to you
  • No photos required: Connect based on interests, not appearance
  • Ghost mode: Appear offline to everyone in Settings
  • Block users: Instantly prevent any user from contacting you
  • Emergency Exit: One tap to leave any situation
  • Encrypted chats: Messages are encrypted in transit
