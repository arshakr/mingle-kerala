Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

At Mingle Kerala, your privacy is not just a legal formality — it is the core principle that everything we build is designed around. We created this platform specifically to allow adults in Kerala to connect anonymously, and we take that responsibility seriously.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you sign in and create a profile, we collect the information needed to provide the service:

  • Email address — used to send sign-in links and recover access to your profile; it is not displayed to other users
  • Self-declared age confirmation — the time you confirmed you are 18 or older
  • Anonymous username, district, gender option, language, and interests — used for your profile and discovery
  • Account and session timestamps — used to maintain sign-in and online status

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, or government ID. Your email address is personal data and is stored with your account; it is not included in public profile responses.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Connection data: A keyed hash of the requesting IP address, temporarily used to limit sign-in-link abuse; raw IP addresses are not stored in the profile
  • LocalStorage data: A display cache of your profile, age confirmation, and preferences

1.3 Chat Messages

The current chat screen contains demo conversations and simulated replies. Do not use it to send sensitive information. Demo messages are not end-to-end encrypted and are not saved as chat history.

2. How We Use Information

  • To match you with other anonymous users based on district and interests
  • To provide profile creation and discovery between adults
  • To enforce our 18+ age requirement and community safety policies
  • To investigate reports of abuse or policy violations
  • To improve our platform features and user experience (anonymized analytics only)
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your email address, verified email status, self-declared 18+ confirmation, chosen anonymous username, district, gender option, language, interests, and account timestamps are stored in a PostgreSQL database. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and can only be used once. Session cookies are HttpOnly and, in production, Secure and SameSite=Lax. Sign-in requests are rate limited; a keyed hash of the requesting IP address is retained with sign-in-link records for up to about one hour, until the hourly expiry cleanup runs. Email delivery is handled by Resend.

A copy of your profile is cached in browser localStorage for display; the server database is the source of truth. Clearing browser data signs out the local display but does not delete your account. Use Delete My Account in Settings to request deletion.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share anonymized, aggregated statistics (e.g., "X users from Kozhikode") for research purposes.

We may disclose information to law enforcement if required by valid Indian legal process (court orders, warrants) for investigation of serious crimes. We will notify you of such requests when legally permitted to do so.

5. Your Rights (under Indian IT Act & DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate information in your profile
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and all associated data via Settings
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Stop using the platform at any time
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Contact our Grievance Officer (details below)

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The API uses a first-party HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. The browser also caches the displayed profile, age-confirmation flag, and preferences in localStorage. No third-party tracking cookies are used. We do not use Google Analytics or cross-site advertising trackers.

7. Age Verification

This platform is strictly for adults 18 years of age and older. By affirming your age during the verification step, you represent and warrant that you are at least 18 years old. We rely on this self-declaration as our primary age verification mechanism. If we have reason to believe a user is underage, we will terminate their access immediately.

8. Anonymous Mode

When "Anonymous Mode" is enabled in Settings, the preference is stored in your browser; it is not yet enforced by the server-side discovery API.

9. Children's Privacy

Mingle Kerala is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a user is under 18, please report them immediately using our in-app reporting feature or contact our Safety Center.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active users of material changes through an in-app notice at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Your continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Grievance Officer

In accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may contact our Grievance Officer:

  • Name: Grievance Officer, Mingle Kerala
  • Email: privacy@minglekerala.in
  • Response time: Within 48 hours of receipt
  • Resolution time: Within 30 days

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact us at: privacy@minglekerala.in

For safety and abuse concerns, visit our Safety Center.
